## The plans

Team is the plan to push; Solo is the entry point, and Business at $199 is for bigger teams (and makes $99 look reasonable).

|   |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|

|Team members and roles|No, just you|Yes, unlimited|Yes, unlimited|

|AI replies a month|About 200|About 750|About 1,500|
|Posting to all accounts at once|Unlimited|Unlimited|Unlimited|

|Leads and complaints flagged to you|No|Yes|Yes|

|Google reviews answered|Yes|Yes|Yes|

**Every plan includes:** every comment, DM (direct message) and review in one inbox; replies drafted in your voice from your business info; a leads list emailed to you; approve-first replies with an 8-second undo; official platform connections, never your password; no contract, cancel any time.

Business is on sale from launch, monthly only until Jason sets a yearly price. It differs from Team by its 10 included accounts, about 1,500 AI replies and the price.

**How follow-ups for quiet leads work** (decided 18 November 2026): the owner writes the follow-up once (for example "Still interested? We have Saturday spots left."). When a lead stops replying, Waggle sends it on its own and stops the moment they answer. It's the owner's own message, not one written by AI for each person, so it costs no AI replies. Instagram and Facebook only allow a business to message someone within 24 hours of their last message, so there the follow-up goes out inside that window; WhatsApp uses an approved template after 24 hours.

## When the AI reply limit runs out

Only AI drafting pauses. Posting, the inbox, replying by hand, and lead and review alerts keep working. The line for the page: "You never go dark. Waggle just stops writing for you until next month."

1. **At 80%:** an email and an in-app note, for example "You've used 160 of 200 replies. Resets on [date]."
2. **At 100%:** drafts pause. A banner on the inbox offers "+200 replies for $15" (one click, charged to the card on file) or "Upgrade to Team", with the reset date.
3. **Automatic top-up, if the customer opted in:** 200 replies for $15 are added on their own, up to 2 times a month, with an email receipt each time.
4. **Upgrade mid-month:** the new limit applies right away.

## Why these prices

Team at $99 sits $60 under the next tool that auto-replies from your info (Presly Pro, $159) and far under Birdeye, and every price keeps at least 59% margin in the worst case. Competitor prices were checked 7 November 2026.
